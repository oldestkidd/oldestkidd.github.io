02 / About

IT graduate. Full-stack builder.

Dar es Salaam, Tanzania · open across East Africa

I'm Jumaa Omari Majani — an IT graduate and full-stack developer based in Dar es Salaam. I work end to end: shaping the data model, building the API, designing the interface and getting it deployed.

My foundation is IT support and data: a BSc in Information Technology from the College of Business Education, frontline support at the High Court of Tanzania, and electronics and systems work at Otex Technologies. That background keeps me close to the people who use the software — and to the reports and records the business depends on. I'm also certified in Data Analytics Essentials by Cisco, with practical Excel, SQL, Power BI and Tableau.

My favourite projects are the unglamorous ones people rely on every day — rent collection, farming advice, bookings and budgets. Much of that work is built for East African conditions: low-bandwidth connections, phones that go offline, prices in TZS and users who need Swahili as much as English. Software only matters when it holds up in the hands of the person using it.

How I work

  1. 01

    Design for the device in hand. Mobile-first, light on data, tolerant of a dropped connection.

  2. 02

    Model the money properly. TZS amounts, exact decimals, attributed payments and immutable records.

  3. 03

    Test the unhappy path. Overlapping bookings, failed payments, expired sessions and empty states.

  4. 04

    Ship it, then keep it running. Clear handover notes, documented setup and a deployment you can repeat.
